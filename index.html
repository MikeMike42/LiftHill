How to play

A mystery roller coaster is waiting at the top of the lift hill. You have six guesses to name it.

Type a coaster name, pick it from the list, and every stat is compared against the answer:

✓ that stat matches the answer.

✕ wrong.

On number tiles, ▲ means the answer is higher than your guess and ▼ means it's lower.

Easy mode (in settings) adds two more clues per guess — track type and opening year — and turns on ~ amber "close" tiles for stats within about 10% (or one inversion).

Hints unlock after your third and fifth guesses.

Modes

Daily gives everyone the same coaster each day. Endless picks a new one whenever you like. Challenge lets you choose the answer and send a friend a link or code to guess it.

About the data

Stats are hand-curated from published ride specifications for about a hundred well-known coasters. Manufacturers and parks sometimes list slightly different figures, so treat the numbers as close rather than gospel.
